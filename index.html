Fahim Ahmed Irfan

Ph.D. Student · Computer Science · Kennesaw State University

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Kennesaw State University. My research sits at the intersection of the Internet of Things (IoT), Cyber‑Physical Systems, Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning, with a focus on building smart, secure and human‑centric environments from IoT sensor time-series data.

I completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science and a Graduate Certificate in Software Engineering at Missouri State University in 2025, where I worked in the Multimedia Systems and Communication (MuSyC) Lab under Dr. Razib Iqbal. Before that I earned my B.Sc. in Naval Architecture and Marine Engineering from BUET and spent four years as a software engineer building telemedicine, learning and HR platforms.

Thesis

Towards Automated Sensor Actuator Mapping for Dynamic Smart Spaces

Missouri State University

An unsupervised approach for mapping sensors to actuators in smart spaces by analyzing time series data of occupant-triggered events. It clusters sensor-actuator pairs based on temporal patterns, eliminating manual rule-setting and proving effective across dynamic real-world deployments. Read on BearWorks
